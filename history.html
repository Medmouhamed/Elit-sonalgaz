Sonelgaz - Company History

From EGA to a National Energy Leader

1946 – Creation of EGA

In 1946, the Electricity and Gas of Algeria (EGA) was established following the nationalization of private electricity and gas companies, marking the beginning of Algeria's organized energy sector.

1969 – Establishment of Sonelgaz

On July 28, 1969, Sonelgaz (National Company for Electricity and Gas) was founded, replacing EGA. It was granted a monopoly over the production, distribution, import, and export of electricity, as well as the distribution and sale of natural gas in Algeria.

1983 – First Restructuring

Sonelgaz underwent its first restructuring in 1983, creating specialized subsidiaries such as KAHRIF (rural electrification), KAHRAKIB (electrical infrastructure), KANAGHAZ (gas networks), INERGA (civil engineering), ETTERKIB (industrial assembly), and AMC (manufacturing of meters and measurement devices).

1995 – Transition to EPIC

In 1995, Sonelgaz became a Public Industrial and Commercial Establishment (EPIC), gaining legal personality and financial autonomy, while remaining under the supervision of the Ministry of Energy and Mines.

2002 – Legal Transformation

Sonelgaz's monopoly was revoked by presidential decree No. 02-195 in 2002, converting it into a joint-stock company (SPA) wholly owned by the Algerian state, aligning with the liberalization of the energy sector.

2011 – Reorganization into a Holding

In 2011, Sonelgaz reorganized into a holding company structure, overseeing multiple subsidiaries to enhance operational efficiency and adaptability in a dynamic energy market.
